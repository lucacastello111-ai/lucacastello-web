Fiction

Retratos del Apocalipsis

Director / Editor

Horror feature written, directed and produced by Luca Castello together with Fabián Forte and Nicanor Loreti. Official selection at Mórbido (Mexico), Mar del Plata (Argentina), Fantaspoa (Brazil) and Curtas (Spain), among others, and winner of Best International Feature at the Medellín Horror Fest (Colombia). An anthology of apocalyptic stories driven by fanaticism, violence and the supernatural. Currently in international distribution.

Corporea

Editor

Feature film directed by Cristian Bidone. An unsettling, stylized story that blurs the line between perception and reality. Winner of Best International Feature at the Apulia Horror Festival (Italy), with screenings at CIMIFF (Mexico), Terror Córdoba (Argentina) and the ETREUM Horror Film Fest (Colombia).

La Piel No Es Un Límite

Director / Editor

Short film that premiered in the international competition of the Macabro Finch Festival in Mexico City. It went on to compete at twenty international festivals and won Best International Short at the 7ème Aaaargh Festival in Belgium.

El Ritual del Nahual

Supervising Editor & Trailer Editor

Horror feature directed by Carlos Matienzo Serment. It had its international premiere at the International Film Festival Rotterdam and was part of the Blood Window Showcase at the Cannes Marché du Film. Released in more than 500 theaters across Mexico in May 2026.

El Amigo Visible

Editor

Feature film directed by Cristian Bidone. Selected at more than 45 festivals in Argentina and abroad, winning Best International Film at the Abbey Film Festival, Best Thriller at Accord Cine Fest and Best Horror Film at Dark Soul Fest.

El Hombre de la Luna

Editor

Feature film directed by Rodrigo Pérez Green, starring brothers Manuel and Rodrigo De La Serna. Selected for the Argentine Competition at the 40th Mar del Plata International Film Festival (2025).

Román

Editor

Feature film directed by Majo Staffolani. It premiered in the Latin American competition at BAFICI and went on to screen in competition at more than 40 festivals, winning the Audience Award for Best Feature at the 33rd BFI Flare London LGBTQ+ Film Festival.

La Amante

Director / Editor

Short film selected at seven international festivals, including the Diorama Film Festival (India), the Penitente Film Fest (Colombia) and The Paus Premieres (United Kingdom).

Golondrinas

Trailer Editor

Feature film directed by Mariano Mouriño and produced by SM Cine. Winner of INCAA's Opera Prima competition and of the top prize at the Construir Cine Festival.

Una noche de amor

VFX Compositor

Feature film produced by Patagonik Film Group, with visual effects by SinSistema.
